📜 About

This is a separate application registry, not a native PoA naming protocol. These notes summarize the app's current rules.

🔑 Ownership

Registry ownership is permanent, with no renewals or expiry. Names can change hands by resale or transfer. Retiring a subdomain requires its holder and parent owner; the retired path stays reserved.

💰 Payments & fees

Registry prices, balances and the automatic onboarding grant are internal accounting units, not POA, wallet money, or redeemable assets. Test records are hidden from the public view, not deleted. Hidden names remain unavailable. Native naming and real-payment new-name purchases are unavailable.

The cart is an unsigned plan, not a checkout. POA escrow supports resale or auctions of existing deeds, not new-name issuance. Review the current price, fees, recipient, amount and unique memo before approving a payment. Funding escrow is not a bid, purchase or title transfer.

↩️ Refunds

Eligible outbid, cancellation and expiry refunds go only to the original payer. Pauses do not erase obligations. There is no automatic refund after an uncertain or verified payout; check the recorded status before retrying anything.

🤖 Agents

The browser does not sign. Copied requests need separate review and approval. Never share private keys, passwords or recovery phrases. Re-check ownership, prices and versions before signing.

🔒 Privacy & search logs

Submitted name searches are stored in private search logs without IP addresses or account identifiers. Query text is saved; do not enter personal information, wallet addresses or keys. Typing alone is not logged.

⚠️ Risks

Availability, uninterrupted service and successful transactions are not guaranteed. Escrow is custodial; payments and registry title transfer are not atomic. Compromised custody can lose funds. No independent security audit is claimed.

⚖️ Disputes

The app has no built-in arbitration, dispute-resolution or key-recovery service.

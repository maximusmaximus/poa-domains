    What happens after your agent sends POA

    1. Finality first: Your agent creates an escrow intent and sends the exact POA total with the unique memo. PoA network finality (K=5 attestations or checkpoint) is required — seen is not credited yet.
    2. Instant permanent mint: Once final, escrow credits your balance and your agent submits issue for each name. Each domain is minted permanently to your address immediately in that step — no auction or wait for treasury payout.
    3. Verify ownership right away: Open each name’s /d/<name> page or Manage domains with your public poa1… address to see your name.
    4. Safety & unused POA: If a name is taken first or checkout is held, nothing is minted for it. Any unused POA credit stays in your escrow balance and can be withdrawn back only to your original sending address.

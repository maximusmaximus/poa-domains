🧾 Handoff

        
Give your agent a clear request — this page does not sign, pay or register a name.

        
  1. 🔍 Find a name and add it to your planning cart.
  2. 🛒 Check each POA amount, then copy the cart request or download its JSON.
  3. 🤖 Give it to your agent. Ask it to check prices and ownership again, preview the action, and ask for your approval before sending anything.

        
Why? Your agent needs the exact name, amount limit and current registry version. A copied request is not a payment or proof of ownership.

        
Advanced: build one unsigned action by hand
For agents and experienced users. Public addresses and data only. Never paste a private key. Machine field names below match the API; times are Unix timestamps, not a countdown.

Answers

❓ Frequently asked questions

Concise answers on the live implementation, pricing, auctions, profiles, and agents.

Where is the first live implementation of PoA Domains?

The first live implementation runs at poadomains.link (Web Explorer, Remote MCP, and POA escrow). This GitHub Pages site is its static, read-only mirror and local tool companion. See the Terms for full boundaries.

How much does a domain cost, and do names expire?

New standard roots cost 100 POA and premium single-emoji roots cost 1,000 POA (one-time checkout). Names are permanent with no renewals or expiry; you can hold, update, transfer, list, or auction them anytime.

Why doesn’t the site connect a wallet or ask for private keys?

Private keys and seed phrases never belong in a website. PoA Domains only produces unsigned public requests and JSON; your agent or local CLI verifies live state and signs locally only after your approval.

How do POA auctions and outbid refunds work?

Queued auctions start their timer only when the first bid arrives. Each bid must beat the current high bid by more than 10%, late bids extend closing by the quiet window, and outbid funds credit back immediately for re-bidding or withdrawal to your original sending address. Once a fixed-price sale is purchased or an auction closes with a winner, escrow pays the seller and transfers title automatically — no second seller handover step is required.

What happens to profile fields when a domain is listed or sold?

Listing or auctioning pauses attached profile fields and records. Ownership transfers reset them cleanly for the new holder while preserving prior entries as read-only history on the domain page.

How do AI agents connect?

Connect over Streamable HTTP MCP at https://poadomains.link/mcp, read discovery metadata at llms.txt, or paste copied Cart / Manage domains prompts from the Explorer.

Why can POA checkout show as paused or held?

Safety checks automatically hold escrow payments when upstream PoA network readings are stale, unreachable, or reconciling. Holds protect funds and never erase balances or refund obligations.

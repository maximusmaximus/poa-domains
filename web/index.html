❓ Frequently asked questions

Quick answers on pricing, security, auctions, profiles, and agents.

What is PoA Domains?

PoA Domains provides permanent text and emoji names, subdomains, and public profiles settled through POA escrow. See the Terms for full details.

How much does a name cost, and do domains expire?

New standard root names cost 100 POA and premium single-emoji names cost 1,000 POA (one-time checkout). Domains are permanent with no renewals or expiry; you can keep, update, transfer, sell, or auction them anytime.

Why doesn’t this site connect a wallet or ask for private keys?

Private keys, seed phrases, and passwords never belong in a website. PoA Domains only builds unsigned public instructions and JSON. Your AI agent or local CLI verifies current prices, ownership, and escrow status, signs locally in your own wallet, and submits only after your approval.

How do POA auctions, timers, and outbid refunds work?

Queued inventory auctions start their countdown timer only when the first bid lands. Each new bid must beat the current high bid by more than 10%, and late bids extend the close time by the quiet window. If another bidder outbids you, your POA returns immediately to your available escrow balance so you can re-bid right away or request a withdrawal back to your original sending address.

What happens to my profile fields and avatar if I list or sell my domain?

While a domain is listed for sale or in an active auction, attached profile fields and records pause so buyers see a clean listing. When ownership transfers to a new holder, previous profile fields and records are dropped for a clean slate and preserved as read-only history on the domain’s page.

How do AI agents connect to PoA Domains?

Agents can connect over Streamable HTTP MCP at https://poadomains.grok.me/mcp, inspect discovery docs at llms.txt, or accept copied prompts and downloaded JSON from the Explorer’s Cart and Manage domains views.

Why can POA payments show as held or paused?

Safety checks automatically pause escrow payments whenever upstream PoA network readings are slow, stale, unreachable, or awaiting reconciliation. Holds never erase balances or refund obligations — your agent simply stops before sending funds until checkout reopens.

What is the difference between this Mirror Site and the Explorer?

This GitHub Pages Mirror Site is a static, read-only overview, public-node telemetry viewer, and local Unicode emoji spelling checker. Open the live PoA Domains Explorer to search names, view domain profiles, join auctions, or manage domains.
